Approve a build with your wallet

A second, independent signature for the signer quorum (--quorum 2): a stolen CI key alone can no longer authorise an image. Your wallet signs one Memo transaction; this page never sees your private key. Wallets that use a Ledger (Phantom, Solflare) work the same way.

Before you approve, compare the digest with the one printed by the CI run of the build you are approving. Approving a digest you did not check defeats the purpose.
